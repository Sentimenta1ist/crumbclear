Cookies and site data are deleted when you close the last tab of a site. Sites on your lists are left alone.

First, pick the sites you want to stay signed in to
Auto-clean is off until you finish. After that, cookies of every other site are deleted once you close its tabs. Google, Microsoft, Apple, GitHub and Okta sign-in are already on your Keep list. Moving from Cookie AutoDelete? Import your list below first.

    Cleaning

    Auto-clean
    Pause to keep everything until you turn it back on.
    Delay after closing a tab
    Time to reopen a tab before its cookies are gone. Several closed tabs are cleaned together.
    Clean when a tab moves to another site
    Navigating away counts as closing the old site.
    Also delete site data
    localStorage, IndexedDB, cache storage and service workers, not just cookies.
    Clean on browser start
    Removes leftovers from the last session, including "until restart" sites. When off, "until restart" sites are kept like Keep.
    Cookie count on the icon
    Blue: kept. Amber: kept until restart. Teal: will be cleaned. Grey: auto-clean is paused.
